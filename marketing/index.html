How it works

Install once. Catch it forever.

  1. Paste your SOW

    One time, in the extension popup. Stored locally on your device — never sent anywhere.

  2. Browse Slack normally

    The extension watches client messages for scope-expansion patterns (190 phrases across 15 categories). Flagged messages get a discrete badge in the corner.

  3. Click → copy → send

    Badge click opens a panel with the trigger quoted, your SOW for context, and a drafted change-order email in your tone of choice. Copy and paste back into Slack.

What it does NOT do

Privacy isn't an afterthought.

No server roundtrip

Your client messages, your SOW, your stats — all stored in your browser's local storage. Nothing leaves your device.

No AI in the request path

Detection is a deterministic lexicon scan, not an LLM. Zero per-message API cost. Zero data sent to any model.

No tracking, no analytics

No third-party scripts, no telemetry, no cookies. Open source — read the code yourself.

No subscriptions for v1

Free during beta. Pro tier ($12/mo) and Agency tier ($39/mo) ship later — fully optional.
