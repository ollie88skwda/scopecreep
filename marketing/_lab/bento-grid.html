COMPONENT · SC-BENTO

How it works in three steps.

Paste your SOW. Browse Slack. Click to draft. The change order takes ten seconds.

Layout: 2-col asymmetric Cards: 1 wide + 2 stacked Mobile: single-column stack Hover: translateY(-2px)

STEP 01

Paste your SOW.

Drop the statement of work into the popup once per project. ScopeCreep extracts the deliverables, the dates, and the explicit out-of-scope items. Everything stays local — your contract never leaves the browser.

  SOW · Acme Co · v1.2
  ────────────────────
  IN  : 4 landing pages
  IN  : copy + design
  OUT : animation
  OUT : video
  OUT : code

STEP 02

Browse Slack.

Work normally. ScopeCreep watches incoming messages against your SOW and the 274-phrase lexicon. When a request crosses the line, a dot appears in the corner.

client: hey can you also
        add a hover state
        to every button?

    ▲ flagged · ADD-001
      severity: medium

STEP 03

Click to draft.

One click drafts a professional change-order reply quoting the original SOW clause. Edit, send, bill what you signed up for. Ten seconds end-to-end.

draft → "Happy to add it!
        Per SOW §2.1, hover
        states weren't in
        the original scope —
        $180 + 1 day."
